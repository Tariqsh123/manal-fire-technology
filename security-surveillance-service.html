Security Surveillance Systems

Security surveillance is the cornerstone of modern safety and loss prevention. Our integrated security solutions combine state-of-the-art CCTV cameras, access control systems, intrusion detection, and remote monitoring to provide complete protection for residential, commercial, and industrial facilities. With our systems, you can monitor your premises in real-time, record critical events, and deter potential threats before they escalate.

At Manal Fire Technology, we design and install customized security surveillance systems tailored to your specific needs. From small retail stores to large industrial complexes, our solutions ensure that you have full visibility and control over your property at all times.

CCTV Camera Installation

Our CCTV camera solutions include high-definition IP cameras, analog cameras, and specialized options like PTZ (Pan-Tilt-Zoom), dome cameras, bullet cameras, and thermal cameras. We provide:

  • HD and 4K resolution cameras for crystal-clear images
  • Night vision and infrared cameras for 24/7 monitoring
  • Wireless and wired camera systems
  • Remote viewing via mobile apps and web interfaces
  • Cloud and local storage options

Access Control Systems

Control who enters your building with our advanced access control solutions. We offer:

  • Biometric fingerprint and facial recognition systems
  • Card and key fob access systems
  • Intercom and video door phone systems
  • Time and attendance integration
  • Remote access management

Intrusion Detection & Alarm Systems

Protect your property against unauthorized entry with our intrusion detection systems:

  • Motion sensors and glass break detectors
  • Door and window contact sensors
  • Perimeter fencing and outdoor detection
  • Integration with CCTV and access control
  • 24/7 professional monitoring services

Security Benefits

Investing in a professional security surveillance system provides numerous benefits:

  • Crime Deterrence: Visible cameras discourage criminal activity
  • 24/7 Monitoring: Continuous surveillance ensures round-the-clock protection
  • Incident Investigation: Recorded footage helps identify perpetrators and resolve disputes
  • Operational Efficiency: Monitor employee productivity and safety compliance
  • Insurance Benefits: Many insurers offer discounts for professionally installed security systems
  • Peace of Mind: Know that your property, employees, and assets are always protected
