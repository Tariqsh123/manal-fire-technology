Our Fire Pump Services

Comprehensive solutions from flow analysis to emergency repair — all backed by NFPA standards.

Initial Water Flow Analysis

We analyze municipal water pressure and site-specific demands to custom-engineer the right pump for your building.

  • Site pressure & flow testing
  • Hydraulic design verification
  • NFPA 20 compliant sizing

New Pump Installation

From horizontal split case to vertical turbine, we install electric and diesel-driven pumps with precision.

  • Turnkey installation & commissioning
  • Controller & transfer switch setup
  • Permit & submittal packages

Retrofitting & Upgrades

Modernize your existing fire pump system to meet current codes and improve reliability.

  • Controller & pressure switch upgrades
  • Motor & diesel engine retrofits
  • Flow test & performance validation

Jockey Pump Services

We service the small pump that maintains system pressure and ensures your main fire pump starts reliably.

  • Jockey pump installation & repair
  • Pressure switch calibration
  • Parallel system integration

Annual Flow Tests

NFPA 25 requires annual flow testing. Our certified inspectors deliver detailed reports with actionable insights.

  • Full flow & churn tests
  • Pressure & flow data logging
  • Comprehensive inspection reports

Diesel Engine Service

Annual and semiannual diesel pump maintenance to ensure your engine starts every time.

  • Fuel system & battery inspection
  • Coolant & exhaust checks
  • Load bank testing

Fire Pump Maintenance & Inspections

Without proper maintenance, a fire pump may not deliver the pressure your building requires. At FirePro, our certified inspectors comply with NFPA 25, NFPA 72, and Pakistan building codes. We provide detailed inspection reports and complete trouble-shooting for pressure switches, gauges, motor repairs, jockey pumps, and fire pump controllers. We also repack and rebuild pumps — all in compliance with NFPA 20, county, and city requirements.

24/7 Emergency Fire Pump Service

365 days a year, our fire sprinkler technicians are equipped and ready to service your fire pump. From alarm conditions to complete pump failure, we respond fast to keep your system operational. All repairs comply with NFPA 20, NFPA 70, and local requirements.
